## 📖 About

**Block Blast Solver** is a free online tool that analyzes your Block Blast puzzle board and suggests the optimal placements for your pieces. It helps you avoid dead ends, maintain combo streaks, survive longer and achieve higher scores — no installation, no signup, just upload and solve.

**🌐 Official Website: <a href="https://blockblastsolversi.com">blockblastsolversi.com</a>**

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 📸 **Screenshot Analysis** — Upload one game screenshot; the 8×8 board and all 3 upcoming pieces are detected automatically
- 🧠 **Advanced Solving Engine** — Every possible placement is evaluated to find the strongest move sequence
- 👣 **Step-by-Step Moves** — Clear visual instructions showing exactly where to place each piece, in order
- ✍️ **Manual Board Editor** — No screenshot? Build your board by hand and solve it the same way
- 🔥 **Combo Protection** — Suggestions that keep your combo streaks alive and your board open
- 📱 **Mobile Friendly** — Works beautifully on phone, tablet and desktop
- 📚 **Free Strategy Guides** — Learn the game with in-depth guides written for real players
- ⚡ **Instant & Free** — Browser-based tool, results in seconds, no download required

---

## 🚀 How It Works

| Step | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| **1. Upload** | Take a screenshot of your Block Blast game and upload it (or fill the board manually) |
| **2. Analyze** | The tool reads your board and the 3 upcoming pieces automatically |
| **3. Solve** | Press Solve and follow the step-by-step placements for the best possible moves |
